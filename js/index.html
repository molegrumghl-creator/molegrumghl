Why this exists

Most bloating advice is either vague or overwhelming.

Cut out gluten. Cut out dairy. Try low-FODMAP. Do a cleanse. Drink celery juice. The advice comes from everywhere and most of it is written for someone with a different digestive system than yours.

Molegru'm GHL exists to bring the noise back down. The idea is simple: keep a short diary, test one food at a time, and pay attention to what your own body does. Not someone else's list — yours.

More about the site

The three ideas behind everything here

  • Foods, not labels. "Processed food" means nothing. Bread, beans, cheese and coffee are things you can actually test.
  • One change at a time. If you change five things, you'll never know which one mattered.
  • Calm beats drastic. Elimination diets have a cost. A little structure goes further than cutting out everything.
