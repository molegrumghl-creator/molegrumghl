Privacy

Privacy policy.

Plain-language, because it should be. Last updated: October 2026.

Who this site is

Molegru'm GHL is a static website at molegrumghl.vercel.app, written by Ronald Aduda. This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit the site or use its forms.

What this site does not collect

The site itself does not set cookies, does not use analytics, and does not run any third-party tracking. There is no ad network and no profile built from your visit.

Email signup

If you join the waitlist, the email address you enter is sent to a mailing-list provider (MailerLite). It is used to send you the update you signed up for. Nothing else.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. Once you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the active list.

You can also ask for your data to be deleted entirely by writing to molegrumghl@gmail.com.

Contact form

The contact form on this site opens your own email app — nothing is submitted to this site's server. The message goes straight from your email account to molegrumghl@gmail.com. What happens to it after that depends on your email provider and on the standard behaviour of email.

Purchases

Purchases of The Molegru'm Gut Reset Protocol are handled entirely by Payhip. Payment details are collected and processed by Payhip, not by this site. For Payhip's own privacy practices, see their policy on payhip.com.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel's servers may log basic request information (such as IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and secure. For details, see Vercel's own privacy policy.

Fonts

This site loads two typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google may see the request. This is standard for sites that use Google Fonts.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask what personal information of yours is held, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted. Email molegrumghl@gmail.com and say what you need.

Not medical advice

Nothing on this site is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is general education. For personal medical concerns, see a qualified clinician.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" line at the top of the page changes too. Substantial changes will be noted on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy: molegrumghl@gmail.com.
